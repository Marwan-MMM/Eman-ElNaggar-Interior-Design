The Studio

Design is a quiet act of memory.

Eman El-Naggar is an interior architect based in Cairo. For over fifteen years she has composed residences across Egypt and the Gulf — from Zamalek heritage apartments to El Gouna lagoon villas — pairing deep, restful palettes with brass, travertine and hand-finished detail.

Every project begins the same way: with listening. Rooms are measured, yes — but so are mornings, gatherings, silences and light. What remains is a home that feels less decorated than inevitable.

— Eman
